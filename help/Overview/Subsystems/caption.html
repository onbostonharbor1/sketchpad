caption.js — The Caption Bar

Rewritten 27 October 2026 against the current code. New since the earlier page: a Reset button option and the Prev/Next click zones over the picture. Three helpers the old page described are still exported but no longer called, and one of them would fail if it were (see the end).

The caption bar is the gray strip above the picture. Every tab builds it the same way, through setCaptionBar(): a title on the left, buttons on the right.

setCaptionBar(config)

configMeaning
targetIdthe container, normally "caption" (required; throws if missing)
titletext on the left
onPrev, onNextif given, Prev and Next buttons
overlayTargetIdwith both of the above: also turns on click zones over that region (left quarter = Prev, right quarter = Next; nextPrevOverlay.js). Without it the zones are turned off.
onPrimaryif given, a Reset button (meant for secondary objects; no tab passes it at present)
onMenu(button, event)what the "v" button does; without it "v" is drawn grayed and inert

The bar is rebuilt from nothing on every call. The "v" button is always there. The title goes in a span.caption-title; the buttons in a div.caption-buttons, in the order Prev, Next, Reset, v. Tabs that add their own items to the bar (Home's file path, Figures' Line and Parabola buttons and layer name) find it by those class names, after building it.

Who uses Prev/Next and the click zones

  • Patterns: buttons, and click zones over the picture.
  • Gallery: buttons, and click zones over the picture (scripts) or the image (in #text).

Exported but unused

  • normalizeCaptionEntry(raw): returns { title, filename, category } from loosely shaped data.
  • rebuildCaption(config): empties the container and calls setCaptionBar, which empties it anyway.
  • buildMenuHandler(tabName, context): returns a handler that calls menuManager.open(tabName, context). That is the wrong argument list for today's open(items, anchor), so it would throw. Nothing calls it; it is a candidate for deletion.
